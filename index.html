THE ACADEMIC TREK EXPERIENCE

An Exclusive Immersion into Indian Cinema

Designed as a premier hands-on masterclass initiative, the Academic Trek invites students behind the velvet curtain into the heart of Bollywood filmmaking at Film City Mumbai. Participants explore world-class studios, understand advanced cinematography and sound design, and engage directly with renowned directors, technicians, and artists.

02 Full Days of Masterclasses
10+ Creative Disciplines
100% Hands-On Studio Access

Key Highlights

Curated learning experiences designed for future media and creative leaders

Film Sets & Studio Tours

Guided walkthroughs of high-tech production soundstages, film sets, chroma suites, and camera facilities in Film City.

Interactive Filmmaker Sessions

Direct Q&A dialogues and panel discussions with top Bollywood directors, screenwriters, producers, and showrunners.

Hands-On Creative Workshops

Practical workshops covering cinematography, directing, screenplay development, audio engineering, and costume design.

Industry Pathways & Insights

Comprehensive guidance on navigating careers across mainstream cinema, OTT platforms, animation, VFX, and creative arts.

Tribute to Iconic Cinema

A cinematic celebration honoring landmark milestones, legendary directors, and timeless cinematic masterpieces of India.

Celebrity & Peer Networking

Live interactions with celebrity guests, esteemed faculty, casting heads, and fellow cinephile students from across India.
