THE ACADEMIC TREK

Immerse into the World of Indian Cinema & Filmmaking

The Academic Trek is an engaging, hands-on learning experience where students explore the creative, technical, and cultural aspects of the Indian film industry. Participants experience the filmmaking environment firsthand, discover behind-the-scenes magic, and learn directly from Bollywood’s industry professionals.
