THE ACADEMIC TREK EXPERIENCE

An Exclusive Immersion into Indian Cinema

Designed as a premier hands-on masterclass initiative, the Academic Trek invites students behind the velvet curtain into the heart of Bollywood filmmaking at Film City Mumbai. Participants explore world-class studios, understand advanced cinematography and sound design, and engage directly with renowned directors, technicians, and artists.

CAMPUS CODE & EVENT PROTOCOLS

Event Guidelines: Do's & Don'ts

Ensure a seamless, safe, and enriching experience across Film City studios and masterclass venues

RECOMMENDED PROTOCOLS

What You Should Do (Do's)

  • ✓
    Carry Valid Student / Govt Photo ID: Bring your original College ID Card or Govt Photo ID (Aadhaar / Driving License / Voter ID) for mandatory security verification at the main campus gate.
  • ✓
    Keep Your Digital QR Pass Handy: Save your Celebrate Cinema entry pass on your phone or download the pass image for swift scanning at the registration and check-in desks.
  • ✓
    Report According to Scheduled Reporting Time: Please report to the campus premises strictly according to your allocated reporting time communicated prior to the event. Plan your arrival at Film City accordingly.
  • ✓
    Wear Decent Outfits: Attendees are requested to wear decent, respectable smart casual attire suitable for an academic film institution and professional media production environment.
  • ✓
    Maintain Silence in Masterclasses: Switch smartphones to silent or vibrate mode inside screening auditoriums, sound recording stages, and masterclass theatres to honor our guest speakers.
  • ✓
    Follow Tour Marshals & Guides: Stay with your designated batch and follow instructions of Whistling Woods faculty coordinators and student marshals at all times.
  • ✓
    Photography in Designated Public Zones: Capture memories, selfies, and reels in public red-carpet backdrops, exhibition corridors, and selfie booths. Tag @whistling_woods on social media!
STRICTLY PROHIBITED

What You Must Avoid (Don'ts)

  • ✕
    No Outside Food, Snacks or Drinks: Outside food and beverages are strictly barred inside the campus to maintain hygiene. Clean drinking water and cafeteria food courts are accessible.
  • ✕
    Strictly No Smoking, Alcohol or Vapes: Whistling Woods is a zero-tolerance educational campus. Cigarettes, e-cigarettes/vapes, alcohol, and intoxicating substances will lead to immediate expulsion.
  • ✕
    No Recording of Screenings & Masterclasses: Audio/video recording, photography, or live streaming during unreleased film screenings or protected industry masterclasses is illegal under copyright laws.
  • ✕
    Do Not Touch Expensive Studio Equipment: High-end production cameras, lighting fixtures, sound mixing consoles, and set props are delicate and must never be touched without direct mentor supervision.
  • ✕
    No Straying into Active Film Sets: Film City is an active commercial film & television shooting zone. Wandering into non-authorized shooting floors or private artist vanity areas is strictly prohibited.
  • ✕
    Zero Littering on Green Eco-Campus: Whistling Woods borders the lush Aarey eco-system. Littering with plastic cups, bottles, or papers is penalized. Use designated recycling bins.
  • ✕
    No Reselling or Pass Sharing: All academic passes are strictly personal, non-transferable, and non-refundable. Impersonation or sharing QR passes will result in entry cancellation.
Security Reminder: Whistling Woods International & Film City security personnel reserve the right of admission. Please cooperate with security bag inspections at entry.
